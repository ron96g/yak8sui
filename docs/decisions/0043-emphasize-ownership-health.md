# Emphasize health in ownership presentations

Show every ownership resource's health with both a symbol and accessible text. Use a green check for current Healthy resources, a red error symbol for current Unhealthy resources, and a yellow question mark for Unknown or stale health. Stale reports must not receive the green or red current-state treatment even when they retain a previously reported status.

Display the compact symbol in graph cards and the symbol with its text label in the table Health column. Tooltips and accessible names preserve the full health label, so color is never the only signal.
